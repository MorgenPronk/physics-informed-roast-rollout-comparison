# Synthetic benchmark cohort

`roast_timeseries_synthetic_p2_only.csv` is a **fully synthetic** cohort of
221 roasts generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_fixture.py` (fixed seed
2000). It is **not** partner telemetry and does **not** reproduce any value
reported in the manuscript.

Its relationship to the production data is disclosed precisely: the generator
is conditioned **only on label-level aggregate statistics** of the production
cohort — per-label roast counts (107/51/63 for P10/P13/P16), modeled-window
lengths, charge / turning-point / endpoint probe-temperature levels, mean
inlet-air temperature, air speed, and drum speed — which are embedded as
constants in the generator script. No per-roast sequence, timestamp, or
identifier derives from partner data: every trajectory is simulated, roast IDs
are `SYNTH_*`, and timestamps use a fictitious year-2000 epoch. The real
de-identified cohort is available from the corresponding author on reasonable
request (see the manuscript's Data Availability section), handled under the
Data Use Agreement with the providing industrial partner.

The generating physics is the manuscript's mechanistic scaffold driven by
synthetic operator-style profiles, with deliberate model-form discrepancies
relative to the scaffold the repository fits, so that every position in the
placement spectrum has genuine structure to learn:

* a state-dependent heat-transfer coefficient (single-closure target);
* an altered moisture-loss exponent and a label-dependent exotherm law with
  quadratic depletion (multi-closure targets);
* an unmodeled drum-thermal-reservoir heat path and a flow-dependent
  inlet-temperature sensor mismatch (structured probe-space error for the
  residual and neural-baseline positions);
* per-roast latent variation in effective mass, charge temperature, and
  initial moisture (the fitted scaffold uses global scalars);
* first-order probe lag and measurement noise.

The recorded probe signal starts at residual drum temperature while the true
bean charge state stays latent, reproducing the metadata-limited
initialization regime studied in the paper.

`scripts/run_synthetic_study.py` retrains the manuscript-winning
configurations on this cohort at the manuscript-canonical budget and reports
held-out rollout R². The qualitative placement ordering of the manuscript is
reproduced (seed 11; manuscript values on the real cohort in parentheses):

| Model class | Synthetic-cohort R² | Real-cohort R² (paper) |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanistic baseline | −3.07 | (−0.44) |
| Single-closure PI | 0.54 | (0.70) |
| Multi-closure PI | 0.71 | (0.94) |
| Neural baseline | 0.85 | (0.97) |

The ordering (baseline fails; single-closure partial repair; multi-closure
stronger repair; neural baseline strongest) matches the paper; the absolute
values do not and are not meant to. Any numbers obtained on this cohort are
properties of the benchmark, not of the industrial process.

Point any script that accepts `--input` at this file, e.g.:

```
python scripts/probe_greybox_bs8.py --input data/synthetic/roast_timeseries_synthetic_p2_only.csv
```
